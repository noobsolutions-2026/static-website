# NotebookLM Video Generation Prompt — Method Domain

Paste the prompt below into NotebookLM (with the Method research pages loaded as sources) to generate an educational video script/overview.

---

You are an expert educational video scriptwriter and content strategist.

I am analyzing a source video with the following details:
- **[VIDEO TITLE]:** How to Research, Learn & Remember Any Domain — Mental Models, Spaced Repetition & Zettelkasten
- **[CONTENT SUMMARY]:** A practical walkthrough of the meta-skill behind durable learning: compressing a complex domain into stable mental models, scheduling reviews with spaced repetition so you study just before you forget, and linking atomic notes Zettelkasten-style to build a network of thought instead of a pile of folders.

Based strictly on this context and the provided YouTube video transcript references, generate a highly structured, engaging educational video script or comprehensive overview. Adhere to these audience retention and pacing rules:

1. THE CRITICAL HOOK (0:00 - 0:15)
- Write a high-impact opening that hooks the viewer within the first 5 to 15 seconds.
- Deliver the core "value proposition" immediately. Do not start with generic intros (e.g., "Welcome back to the channel").
- Clearly state what problem will be solved or what fascinating concept will be learned based on the [VIDEO TITLE].

2. TARGET DURATION & PACING (3 - 10 Minutes Total)
- Structure the content to fit an optimal educational sweet spot of 3 to 10 minutes.
- Maintain rapid, crisp pacing. Every sentence must deliver new, actionable information drawn from the transcripts. Avoid fluff or repetitive summaries.

3. RETENTION-DRIVEN STRUCTURE
Divide the main body of the summary into clear, scannable segments using this format:
- Segment Title: Use bold, punchy names.
- Core Insight: A one-sentence takeaway.
- Key Points: A short, bulleted list of facts, steps, or evidence directly from the source references.
- Engagement Prompt: A rhetorical question or "did you know?" fact to break up the text and maintain viewer focus.

4. AUDIENCE RETENTION OPTIMIZATION
- At the mid-point of the overview, include a "Retention Reset"—a sudden twist, counter-intuitive fact, or crucial warning from the source to prevent the typical 30%–60% viewer drop-off.
- End with a strict "No-Fluff Conclusion" that outlines a direct call-to-action or immediate next step for the viewer, ending the video sharply to maintain high completion rates.

Constraints: Rely primarily on the uploaded transcript references to fill in the details. Use ONLY information explicitly stated in the sources. Do not introduce outside knowledge or extrapolate.
