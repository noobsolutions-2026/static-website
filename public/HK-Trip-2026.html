✅ Bridge Confirmed Operating (2026) The Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge (HZMB) is fully operational, running 24 hrs/day with shuttle buses every 5–15 minutes.

✅ Option A · Recommended — HZMB Shuttle Bus
Via Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge (港珠澳大橋)
Most convenient from the airport hotel. The HKBCF (HK Boundary Crossing Facility) is on the same Lantau artificial island as the airport — a short bus ride or walk from your hotel.
Your hotel is right next to HKBCF — no need to travel into the city. Buses run every 5–15 min all day. Total journey ~2–2.5 hrs door-to-door. Aim to depart 8:30 AM to reach Chimelong by ~11 AM.

④ Clear China Immigration at Zhuhai Port · Hengqin area (allow 15–20 min)
⑤ Zhuhai Port → Chimelong Penguin Hotel: Taxi or Didi · ~20–30 min · ~CNY 50–80 · Or ask Chimelong Hotel if a free shuttle runs
💰 ~HKD 109/adult + taxi · Child fare varies (some free under 1.2m height)

HK's iconic family theme park: giant panda exhibit, marine aquarium with sharks and rays, a cable car ride within the park, and family-friendly rides. Start with the Old HK Street section on ground level — charming recreated street scenes great for photos.

✅ Option A · Recommended
Sheung Wan Old Streets + Central–Mid-Levels Escalator
Wander Sheung Wan's old lanes (antique shops, dried seafood stores, incense, street art). Then hop on the Central–Mid-Levels Escalator — the world's longest outdoor escalator system, a surprisingly fun and unique ride up the hillside.
Uniquely Hong Kong. The escalator is a free, fun ride that young children love. It's also the natural route up towards the Peak Tram, so it flows directly into the afternoon activity.
⚠️ Pram manageable with care — the escalator itself is wide and pram-friendly. However, some streets in Sheung Wan (especially the antique lane area) have steep inclines and uneven stone paving. Stick to Hollywood Road and Queen's Road Central, which are flat and accessible. Avoid the steep side alleys.
